Our mission also includes support for young mothers, elderly people and vulnerable families. These areas remain part of FGR’s long-term commitment as our programs develop.

The heart behind the name

Grande Royale began with one woman’s way of loving people.

Fondation Grande Royale was named in honor of a beloved grandmother remembered for helping people whenever she could and wearing her heart on her sleeve.

Her example became the blueprint: love people, protect their dignity, and help whenever you can.
