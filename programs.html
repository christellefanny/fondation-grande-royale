Our Work

Fondation Grande Royale is currently focused on education and skills development for vulnerable children and young people, while building toward a broader set of programs over time.

01EducationCurrent focus

Helping vulnerable children and young people access learning opportunities, school support, educational materials and guidance that can strengthen their future.

Education supportSchool access, fees and learning materials.
School suppliesBooks, notebooks, uniforms and essential school items.
Learning activitiesWorkshops, literacy support and structured educational sessions.
Guidance & developmentEncouragement, mentorship and opportunities to discover talents and interests.
02Skills & Vocational TrainingCurrent focus

Practical learning, career exploration and vocational skills designed to help disadvantaged young people identify their strengths and build pathways toward greater independence.

Talent discoveryHelping young people identify strengths, interests and abilities.
Career awarenessIntroducing professions, opportunities and possible pathways.
Practical skillsBuilding useful skills that can support future training and employment.
Future planningHelping young people define next steps and build confidence in their plans.
Long-term vision

Programs we are building toward.

As the foundation grows, FGR plans to expand its work into additional areas of protection, care, health access, reintegration and support.

Child Protection & Care

Support for orphans, disadvantaged, abandoned and street-connected children, with a focus on safety, dignity and stability.

Healthcare Access

Improving access to basic care and health support for vulnerable children, young mothers and elderly people.

Young Mothers Empowerment

Support, skills, reintegration and pathways toward greater confidence and economic autonomy.

Elderly Support

Dignity, social connection, health access and participation in community life for older adults.

Children's Multifunctional Center

A proposed facility intended to support the development of disadvantaged and orphaned children.

Reception & Reintegration Center

A proposed center for abandoned young mothers and street-connected children, focused on social reintegration.
